## 1. Hero Product Video

Orange rim lights illuminate the fabric edges. Soft smoke moves in the background. An original orange and white streetwear graphic appears on the shirt through a smooth print-reveal animation. Realistic cotton fabric movement, luxury fashion commercial style, clean product focus, no people, no copyrighted logos. Format 16:9 desktop and 9:16 mobile. Duration 8 seconds. Frame rate 24 or 30 FPS. Seamless loop.

# Brand Customization

Edit business details in `config/site.ts` first, then mirror required runtime changes in `script.js` for this static HTML edition.

Replace placeholders before public launch:

- Verified testimonials
- Final product pricing
- Fabric composition and GSM
- Delivery timing and fees
- Opening hours
- Social media profile URLs
- Legal privacy and terms copy

Use the Driftwear palette: black `#080808`, charcoal `#151515`, dark grey `#232323`, orange `#FF7A00`, golden orange `#FF9D1A`, off-white `#F4F1EA`, light grey `#D9D9D9`, optional electric blue and deep red.

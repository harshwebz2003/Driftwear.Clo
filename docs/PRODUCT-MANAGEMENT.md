# Product Management

Demo product data lives in `script.js` inside the `products` array. Each object includes id, slug, name, category, descriptions, price placeholders, colors, sizes, fit, fabric, GSM placeholder, image paths, video path, customization flags, print locations, featured flags, stock placeholder, rating placeholder, review count and care instructions.

Before launch:

- Replace demo prices with confirmed prices.
- Replace editable fabric and GSM placeholders with verified supplier data.
- Replace mock stock with real inventory.
- Replace demo product images with generated or photographed assets.
- Keep product slugs stable once indexed by search engines.
